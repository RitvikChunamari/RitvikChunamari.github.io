## Product Purpose

The digital portfolio and interactive exhibition of **Ritvik Chunamari**. It showcases design craft, creative technology experiments, and sensory user interfaces that bridge computational systems and human perception.

## Positioning

> **"Creative Designer and Vibe Coder crafting sensory digital interfaces at the intersection of computational logic and human intuition."**

The portfolio merges Swiss typographic minimalism (sharp grids, high-density telemetry, monumental display typography) with creative technology (fluid dynamics, kinetic type, magnetic microinteractions). Vibe coding is presented as an accelerated prototyping capability grounded in rigorous interface architecture.
