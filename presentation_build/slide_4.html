Where It Broke

Figma mockups are easy. Real browsers gave me three big headaches.

✕ Problem 01
Severe Scroll Stutter
Adding 3D card tilt dropped scroll speeds below 30fps. It felt laggy and unpolished.
✕ Problem 02
Mobile Touch Conflict
The cursor particles got in the way of mobile thumb scrolling and drained battery.
✕ Problem 03
Name Wrapping
On small iPhone screens, "Chunamari" broke awkwardly onto a third line.
